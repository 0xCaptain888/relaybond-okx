# RelayBond v0.2.1 — Pending-safe settlement capture

Released: September 15, 2026

- Records the first real 0.01 Testnet USD₮0 Agentic Wallet settlement without overstating it as an accepted delivery.
- Follows an initially pending facilitator receipt to its X Layer transaction.
- Requires the exact token, payer, payee and amount in the onchain ERC-20 `Transfer` event.
- Preserves the merchant delivery body and performs independent RelayBond verification after settlement.
- Updates the public judge console to distinguish a confirmed settlement from complete paid-delivery evidence.
